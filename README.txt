FALAWLA BEAUTY STORE
--------------------
Open index.html in your browser. Internet is required for Bootstrap, Google Fonts, image URLs and the hero video.

IMPORTANT: Before launch, edit CONFIG at the top of script.js:
 whatsapp: Egyptian international number, digits only, e.g. 201XXXXXXXXX
 instagram / facebook: the brand's profile URLs

Products are editable in the products array in script.js. Categories:
 makeup, skincare, accessories, perfume, gifts

This is a front-end demo storefront. Product photos/videos are remote assets and should be replaced with brand-owned product media before production. Checkout opens WhatsApp with cart details and customer form information; there is no payment gateway or server-side order storage.
